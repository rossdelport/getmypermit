How-to guide

How to lodge a building permit online in WA

Short answer

Download a BA1 or BA2 form from the Building and Energy website, then lodge it with your plans through your council's online portal and pay the fees. Council decides within 10 business days (BA1) or 25 business days (BA2).

Last updated 16 November 2026

Before you start

Every council in WA uses the same state forms, but each council has its own online portal. Have these ready before you log in:

  • The right form from the Building and Energy website: a BA1 (certified, with a BA3 certificate from a building surveyor) or a BA2 (uncertified, for houses and Class 10 work like sheds, patios and carports only). Not sure? Use the which BA form checker.
  • Plans as PDFs: usually a site plan (at least 1:200), elevations and a floor plan (at least 1:100), and structural engineering where needed.
  • The estimated cost of the work, because fees are worked out from it.
  • A Construction Training Fund receipt if the work is over $100,000.

Step by step

  1. Find your council's portal (see the table below).
  2. Create an account. First-time users need to register; most portals let you track the application afterwards.
  3. Choose "building permit" and the right application type (BA1 or BA2).
  4. Upload the signed form and your documents. Make sure every section is signed. Incomplete applications can be refused.
  5. Pay the fees. Some councils take payment in the portal; others email you a tax invoice.
  6. Answer any requests for more information quickly, as the clock can stop while council waits.
  7. Wait for the decision: up to 10 business days for a BA1 and 25 business days for a BA2.

Common reasons for delays

  • Unsigned forms or missing owner signatures.
  • No site plan, or one that doesn't show setbacks and levels.
  • No engineering for a structure that needs it.
  • Planning approval needed first: a building permit can't be issued until it's granted.

Questions

Common questions

Can I apply for a building permit online in WA?

Yes. Most councils take applications through their own online portal, using the state's BA1 or BA2 forms from the Building and Energy website.

What do I need to lodge a building permit online?

The signed BA1 or BA2 form, plans as PDFs (site plan, elevations, floor plan), engineering where needed, the estimated cost, and a CTF receipt if the work is over $100,000.

How long does an online building permit take?

Up to 10 business days for a certified BA1 and 25 business days for an uncertified BA2, once the application is complete.

Can I still lodge a building permit in person?

Some councils allow it. The City of Belmont, for example, accepts applications in person or by mail as well as online.
