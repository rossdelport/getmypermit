Council guide

City of Rockingham building permits

Short answer

The City of Rockingham decides a certified BA1 in 10 business days and an uncertified BA2 in 25 business days. You can lodge online through Rock Port. On Special Residential or Special Rural land, you need planning consent first.

Last updated 22 November 2026

BA1 (certified)BA2 (uncertified)
Who checks the plansA private building surveyor, who signs a Certificate of Design Compliance (BA3) firstThe City's building surveyors, who issue the BA3 with the permit
What you lodgeBA1, BA3 and the drawings listed on the BA3BA2, site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, construction details and engineering

All commercial building permits in Rockingham must be certified (BA1).

What needs a permit

The City lists fences, pools, spas and barriers, garden sheds, verandahs, gazebos, garages, workshops, pergolas, houses, retaining walls and changes to ground levels (cut and fill). Some small work is exempt under the state Building Regulations 2012, such as a freestanding shed of 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or less high.

If the work is worth more than $20,000, the permit is issued to a registered builder or an approved owner-builder. Since 1 July 2026, the limit for a shed, garage or carport is $50,000.

What goes in a BA2

  • A site plan at 1:200 or larger, with spot levels, all existing buildings, boundary setbacks, any septic system and a north point.
  • Floor plans, elevations and sections at 1:100, with all dimensions.
  • Construction details showing all materials and sizes.
  • Structural details designed and certified by a practising structural engineer.

Fees are paid when you lodge. You can lodge online through the City's Rock Port portal, in person at the Civic Boulevard office, or by post.

Planning approval first?

Most houses and home projects on residential lots only need a building permit if they meet the R-Codes. But any development on Special Residential or Special Rural land needs Town Planning consent first, including changes to a building envelope. Commercial, industrial and three or more grouped dwellings also need a development application.

Time limits and after you build

  • A permit usually lasts 2 years. Rockingham's page says it's void if work hasn't started within 12 months.
  • The builder must lodge a BA7 notice of completion within 7 days of finishing.
  • Copies of old building plans cost $60 for a residential set or $25 per plan, and can take up to 10 working days.

Already built without a permit?

Building without a permit can bring a penalty of up to $50,000 for a first offence. To fix it, you lodge a BA13 building approval certificate application with a BA18 certificate of building compliance from a registered building surveyor. On rural and special zones you need retrospective planning approval first.

See which BA form you need, how to check if a building is approved, or retrospective approval. Call the City on 9528 0333.

Questions

Common questions

How long does a building permit take in Rockingham?

10 business days for a certified BA1 and 25 business days for an uncertified BA2, once the City has everything it needs.

Can I lodge a building permit online in Rockingham?

Yes, through the City's Rock Port portal. You can also lodge in person or by post.

How long is a Rockingham building permit valid?

Usually 2 years from the date it's issued, unless it says otherwise.

Do I need planning approval in Rockingham?

On Special Residential and Special Rural land, yes, for any development. On normal residential lots, usually not if you meet the R-Codes.
