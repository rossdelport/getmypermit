City of Swan building permits: how to apply

Short answer

Lodge a BA1 (certified by a private building surveyor) or a BA2 (checked by the City), preferably online. The City has 10 business days to decide a BA1 and 25 business days for a BA2, counted from when your application is complete. If they ask for more information, you have 21 days to provide it.

Last updated 7 November 2026

Do you need a building permit in the City of Swan?

The City of Swan follows the same state rules as every WA council. Most new buildings and many additions need a building permit before work starts. Some small structures are exempt, such as a freestanding garden shed of 10 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high. Check first with our list of what doesn't need a permit.

A building permit is separate from planning approval. If your project also needs planning approval, get that first.

Which form to use

Time the City has to decide10 business days25 business days

The clock starts at midnight the day after the City receives a complete application. Not sure which suits you? Read BA1 vs BA2.

How to lodge

  • Online through the City's ePathway portal. The City says online is its preferred way.
  • By email to the City's lodgement address, in person at 2 Midland Square, Midland, or by post.

You can track your application online. The tracker shows key milestones, not every step.

If the City asks for more information

You must send it within 21 days, or the application may be refused. An incomplete application may also be refused, and the City says fees are unlikely to be refunded. So it pays to get the paperwork right the first time.

Government fees

These are set by the state and are usually the same at every council:

FeeAmount
BA1 permit (house or Class 10)0.19% of the value, at least $121
BA2 permit0.32% of the value, at least $121
Building Services Levy$61.65 for work up to $45,000; 0.137% above that

Work out yours with the building permit fee calculator. The City's own schedule of fees covers other charges, like copies of old plans.

After the permit

Build to the approved plans, and lodge a notice of completion (form BA7) within 7 days of finishing. If the City refuses your application, you can appeal to the State Administrative Tribunal.

Questions

Common questions

How long does a City of Swan building permit take?

The City has 10 business days to decide a certified (BA1) application and 25 business days for an uncertified (BA2) one. The clock starts the day after a complete application is received.

How do I apply for a building permit in the City of Swan?

Online through the City's ePathway portal (its preferred way), or by email, in person at 2 Midland Square, Midland, or by post.

How much is a building permit in the City of Swan?

State fees apply: 0.19% of the value for a BA1 or 0.32% for a BA2, at least $121, plus the Building Services Levy of $61.65 for work up to $45,000.

What happens if my City of Swan application is missing information?

You have 21 days to provide it, or the application may be refused. The City says fees are unlikely to be refunded for refused incomplete applications.

Can I appeal a City of Swan building permit refusal?

Yes. You can lodge an appeal with the State Administrative Tribunal.
