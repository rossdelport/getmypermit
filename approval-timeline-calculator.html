How long will my building approval take?

Short answer

In WA a council must decide a complete BA1 building permit within 10 business days and a BA2 within 25 business days. If you also need planning approval, add 60 days, or 90 days if neighbours are consulted. Answer up to three questions below.

Last updated 12 November 2026

How long will my approval take?

Up to three quick questions.

The time limits at a glance

ApprovalTime limit
Building permit, BA1 (certified)10 business days
Building permit, BA2 (uncertified)25 business days
Supplying more information if askedUsually 21 days
Planning approval, no consultation60 days
Planning approval, with consultation90 days

These limits come from the state rules, so they're the same across WA. Councils such as Swan, Melville and Stirling publish them.

When the clock starts

The clock only starts once the council has a complete application. The City of Swan says it starts at midnight the day after a complete application is received. A missing form, plan or fee can add weeks before anything happens.

What slows things down

  • Missing documents. The council asks for more information, and the clock pauses.
  • Not replying in time. If you miss the 21 days, the City of Swan says it may refuse the application, and fees are unlikely to be refunded.
  • Planning approval. It must come first, and it's the longest step.
  • Surveyor turnaround. For a BA1, the private surveyor's time comes before the 10 days.

Not sure which form to use? Read BA1 vs BA2, or planning approval vs building permit.

Questions

Common questions

How long does a building permit take in WA?

A council must decide a complete BA1 within 10 business days and a complete BA2 within 25 business days.

How long does planning approval take in WA?

Usually up to 60 days, or up to 90 days if the council advertises it to neighbours.

Does the clock stop if the council asks for more information?

Yes. You usually get 21 days to supply it. Councils such as Swan may refuse the application if you don't.

Can I lodge the building permit and planning approval together?

Councils such as Wanneroo say planning approval must come before the building permit.
