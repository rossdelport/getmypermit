Council guide

City of Joondalup building permits

Short answer

In Joondalup, small freestanding sheds and patios up to 10 m² and 2.4 m, and pergolas up to 20 m², usually don't need a building permit. Most other projects do. Lodge online; the City decides in 10 working days (certified) or 25 (uncertified).

Last updated 14 November 2026

What needs a building permit in Joondalup

ProjectNo building permit if
ShedFreestanding, 10 m² or less, 2.4 m or less, installed to the maker's instructions
Patio or louvre roofStandalone, 10 m² or less, 2.4 m or less
Pergola20 m² or less and 2.4 m or less
Shade sailStandalone, 20 m² or less, 2.4 m or less (attached sails always need one)
Carport, garage, granny flatAlways needs a building permit

If your project also needs planning approval, Joondalup says the building permit can only be issued after planning approval.

How to apply

  1. Check planning first. Most sheds, patios and pergolas on house lots don't need planning approval if they meet the R-Codes and city policy.
  2. Pick certified or uncertified. A certified application (BA1) comes with a Certificate of Design Compliance from a private building surveyor. For an uncertified one (BA2), the City checks the plans.
  3. Use the right checklist. Joondalup has checklists for each project type, listing the plans and documents it needs.
  4. Lodge online through the City's portal (preferred) or by mail. You'll get an invoice for the fee.
  5. Answer any questions. If something is missing, you usually have 21 days to supply it.

Once the application is complete, the City decides a certified application within 10 working days and an uncertified one within 25 working days. When the work is finished, the builder must lodge a BA7 notice of completion within 7 days.

Questions

Common questions

How long does a building permit take in Joondalup?

Once the application is complete, 10 working days for a certified (BA1) application and 25 working days for an uncertified (BA2) one.

Do I need a permit for a small shed in Joondalup?

Not if it's freestanding, 10 m² or less, 2.4 m or less, and installed to the manufacturer's instructions.

How much does planning approval cost in Joondalup?

Up to $147 for work up to $50,000, and up to 0.382% of the cost from $50,000 to $500,000. Work already done attracts an extra penalty of twice the fee.

Can I lodge a building permit online in Joondalup?

Yes. The City prefers online lodgement through its portal. You can also lodge by mail.
