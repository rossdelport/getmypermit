Owner-builder checker for WA

Short answer

In WA you need owner-builder approval if the work needs a building permit, you're not using a registered builder, and it's worth more than $20,000 (or $50,000 for a shed, garage or carport). Approval usually takes 6 to 8 weeks, and you can only do it once every 6 years.

Last updated 19 November 2026

Do I need owner-builder approval?

Answer four questions. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The two dollar limits

Type of workOwner-builder approval needed if the work is over
House, extension, detached granny flat$20,000
Shed, garage, carport (Class 10a)$50,000

These limits only matter if the work needs a building permit and you're not using a registered builder. You can't split one job into smaller jobs to stay under the limit.

Who can be an owner-builder

  • You must own the land (or have a suitable lease), shown by a Certificate of Title or purchase contract from the last 3 months.
  • You must apply as a person, not a company or trust.
  • You need to show you know the job: usually by finishing an owner-builder course, or through past building registration. You also need a white card or blue card.
  • You can't have had an owner-builder permit in the last 6 years, unless that's waived.
  • You can't plan to sell or rent the property straight away. For a house, you must plan to live there.

A granny flat that shares walls or a roof with your house isn't a "detached house", so it doesn't fit the owner-builder rules for houses.

After approval

As owner-builder you're responsible for the work for 7 years. If you sell within 7 years, you usually need home indemnity insurance that covers the buyer. You must still use licensed electricians and plumbers.

Questions

Common questions

When do I need owner-builder approval in WA?

When the work needs a building permit, you're not using a registered builder, and it's worth more than $20,000, or more than $50,000 for a shed, garage or carport.

How long does owner-builder approval take?

Usually about 6 to 8 weeks. Once it's granted, you have 6 months to apply for your building permit.

How often can I be an owner-builder in WA?

Once every 6 years, unless the state agrees to waive that rule.

Can I build a granny flat as an owner-builder?

Yes, if it's detached from the house. A granny flat that shares walls or a roof with the house doesn't fit the owner-builder rules for houses.
