City of Armadale building permits

Short answer

In the City of Armadale, new homes, granny flats, structural additions, room conversions and re-roofing a house always need a building permit. The City decides in 10 business days (BA1) or 25 (BA2), and a permit lasts 2 years. Sheds, patios and carports depend on size.

Last updated 25 November 2026

What always needs a building permit

The City of Armadale says you always need a building permit for:

  • a new house,
  • a new granny flat,
  • structural changes or additions to a house,
  • turning a shed, garage or carport into a room you live in, and
  • replacing a house roof.

Patios, pergolas, shade sails, sheds, garages, carports, decks, retaining walls, earthworks and water tanks may need one, depending on size and height. The City has an info sheet and checklist for each. Rooftop solar on a house usually doesn't need a permit, unless the roof needs strengthening. A front fence that meets the City's Fencing Local Law 2011 doesn't need one either. Sheds can't go in the front yard.

The form must be filled in fully and signed by the builder and the applicant. Work worth over $100,000 also needs a Construction Training Fund levy receipt.

If you don't meet the R-Codes

You can apply to vary the R-Codes. If that's approved, you still need a building permit before you start. Some jobs also need planning or health approval first.

After the permit

  • A permit lasts 2 years. You can apply for more time.
  • The builder must send a BA7 notice of completion within 7 days of finishing.

You can apply online, by email to info@armadale.wa.gov.au, by post or at 7 Orchard Avenue, Armadale. Building Services is on 9394 5000. See which BA form you need, the BA7 notice and the owner-builder checker.

Questions

Common questions

What needs a building permit in the City of Armadale?

Always: new houses, granny flats, structural additions, converting a shed or garage into a room, and a new house roof. Patios, sheds, carports and decks may need one depending on size.

How long does Armadale take to issue a building permit?

Up to 10 business days for a certified (BA1) application and up to 25 business days for an uncertified (BA2) one.

How long is an Armadale building permit valid?

Two years from the date it's granted. You can apply to extend it.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Armadale?

Yes. The City says replacing a house roof always needs a building permit.
