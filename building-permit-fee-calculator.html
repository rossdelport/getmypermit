WA building permit fee calculator

Short answer

A WA building permit costs 0.32% of the value for a BA2 or 0.19% for a BA1 (at least $121), plus the Building Services Levy ($61.65 up to $45,000). Enter your value below to see the total.

How the fees are worked out

ChargeRate
BA2 (uncertified)0.32% of the value, at least $121
BA1 (certified), Class 1 and 100.19% of the value, at least $121
BA1 (certified), Class 2 to 90.09% of the value, at least $121
Building Services Levy$61.65 up to $45,000; 0.137% of the value above that
Construction Training Fund levy0.2%, only for work over $100,000 starting on or after 1 July 2026

These are set by the state, so they're the same at every WA council.

What counts as the value

The Building Regulations say the value is the total of all goods, labour, services, fees, overheads and profit margin, including GST. Councils can question a value that looks too low.

What the calculator leaves out

  • Council extras, like verge bonds (for example Wanneroo's $140 fee plus a $2,200 bond for work of $20,000 or more)
  • Planning approval, if your project needs it ($147 for work up to $50,000 at councils like Melville)
  • Surveyor, drafting and engineering fees

Questions

Common questions

How do I calculate my building permit fee in WA?

For a BA2, multiply the value of the work by 0.32% (at least $121), then add the Building Services Levy: $61.65 for work up to $45,000, or 0.137% above that. Add 0.2% CTF levy if the work is over $100,000.

What is the minimum building permit fee in WA?

$121, for both BA1 and BA2 applications.

Is the BA1 fee cheaper than BA2?

Yes, 0.19% instead of 0.32% for houses and Class 10 structures, but you also pay a private building surveyor.

Does the fee include GST in the value?

Yes. The value of the work must include GST, along with materials, labour, services, fees, overheads and profit.
