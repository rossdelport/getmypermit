How it works

A simple hand-off.

01

Send us the job

Your customer's details, the site address and your product drawings. One email is enough.

02

We prepare and lodge

Site plan, forms, owner consent, engineering and surveyor certificates where needed, then lodgement.

03

You get the permit

We answer council's questions and send the permit to you and your customer the day it is issued.

Questions

Common questions

Which businesses do you work with?

Shed and garage suppliers, patio and pergola builders, carport companies, granny flat builders, tiny home makers and pool and plunge pool suppliers across Perth and WA.

Can you use our standard drawings and engineering?

Yes. If you have standard plans or engineering certificates for your products, we use them on every job, which keeps each application quick and consistent.

Who does the customer deal with?

Whatever suits you. We can deal with your customer directly and keep you updated, or work only through you.

How do you charge?

Our public prices with 10 to 15% off for regular work, invoiced monthly. Sheds, patios and carports are normally $990, so partners pay from about $840 a job. See all prices.
