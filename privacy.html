Privacy

Privacy

Short answer

We only collect what we need to quote and to handle your application, we only share it with the people working on your job, and we never sell it.

Last updated 29 October 2026

What we collect

When you ask for a quote we collect the details you enter: your name, phone, email, the property address and information about your project. If you email us photos or plans, we keep those too.

How we use it

We use your details to quote, to prepare and lodge your application, and to keep you updated. To do that we may share them with your council, and with a building surveyor, engineer or draftsperson working on your job. We do not sell your details.

How the form works

Our quote form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, an email form service. Our websites are hosted on GitHub Pages and use Google Fonts.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by calling 0400 369 865. Some records may need to be kept while an application is open.
