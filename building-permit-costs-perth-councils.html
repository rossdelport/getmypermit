Cost guide

Building permit costs at Perth councils (2026)

Short answer

The building permit fee is set by the state, so it's the same at every Perth council: 0.32% of the value for a BA2 (at least $121), plus a $61.65 levy for work up to $45,000. A $20,000 shed or patio costs $182.65. Councils differ only on extras, like verge bonds.

Last updated 3 November 2026

The fee is the same at every council

Building permit fees are set by the state's Building Regulations 2012, not by each council. So a BA2 for a $30,000 patio costs the same in Joondalup as it does in Rockingham. What changes between councils are the extras, like verge bonds and planning fees.

ChargeAmount
BA2 (uncertified) application0.32% of the value, at least $121
BA1 (certified) application, houses and Class 100.19% of the value, at least $121
Building Services Levy$61.65 for work up to $45,000; 0.137% of the value above that
Construction Training Fund levy0.2% of the value, only for work over $100,000

The value is the full cost of the work including GST: materials, labour, services, fees, overheads and profit.

Other councils have their own verge, crossover and inspection charges, listed in their yearly fees and charges schedule.

If you need planning approval too

Planning (development) approval is a separate fee. At councils we've checked, such as Subiaco and Melville, it's $147 for work up to $50,000. It's only needed when your project doesn't meet the standard planning rules, or is in a heritage area.

Questions

Common questions

How much is a building permit in WA?

For a BA2, 0.32% of the value of the work, at least $121, plus the Building Services Levy ($61.65 for work up to $45,000). A $20,000 shed or patio costs $182.65 in permit fee and levy.

Are building permit fees different at each Perth council?

No. The permit fees and the levy are set by the state, so they're the same everywhere. Extras like verge bonds, planning fees and inspection fees vary by council.

What is the Building Services Levy?

A state levy collected with your building permit: $61.65 for work up to $45,000, or 0.137% of the value above that.

When do I pay the Construction Training Fund levy?

Only for work over $100,000 that starts on or after 1 July 2026. It's 0.2% of the value.
