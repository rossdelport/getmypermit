City of Gosnells building permits

Short answer

The City of Gosnells decides a certified BA1 in 10 business days and an uncertified BA2 in 25 business days. Small freestanding structures up to 10 m² and 2.4 m are exempt. Lodge online, and have planning, health and owner-builder approvals sorted first if you need them.

Last updated 20 November 2026

Pools and spas 300 mm or deeper always need a permit. Being exempt from a building permit doesn't mean you can skip planning approval or the building rules.

How to apply

  • BA1 (certified): for any building, with a Certificate of Design Compliance (BA3) from a registered building surveyor. The City has 10 business days to decide. The City also offers its own certification service.
  • BA2 (uncertified): only for houses (Class 1a) and sheds, patios and carports (Class 10). The City checks the plans and has 25 business days.

Lodge online through the City's portal with one copy of each document, plus a verge permit application if you'll use the verge. Incomplete applications aren't accepted. If the City asks for more information, you have 21 days to provide it.

Before you lodge

Any other approvals must already be in place: planning approval if needed, health approval for a septic system, and owner-builder approval if you're managing work over $20,000 on a house (or over $50,000 for a shed, patio or carport) without a registered builder.

Planning approval is only needed for a house or minor structure if it breaks the R-Codes' standard rules, a local development plan or a local planning policy. The City decides within 60 days, or 90 with neighbour comment.

After you build

Lodge a BA7 notice of completion within 7 days of finishing, with proof of any inspections the permit asked for. There are penalties if you don't.

Questions

Common questions

How long does the City of Gosnells take to approve a building permit?

10 business days for a certified BA1, or 25 business days for an uncertified BA2, once the application is complete.

What can I build in Gosnells without a permit?

A freestanding patio, shed or gazebo up to 10 m² and 2.4 m high, an open pergola with a house up to 20 m², a dividing fence up to 2 m, and a rainwater tank up to 5,000 litres, among others.

Can I use a BA2 in Gosnells?

Yes, for a house (Class 1a) or a shed, patio or carport (Class 10). Other buildings need a certified BA1.

When do I lodge the BA7 in Gosnells?

Within 7 days of finishing the work, with proof of any required inspections.
