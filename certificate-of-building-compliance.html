How to get a certificate of building compliance (BA18)

Short answer

A BA18 is a certificate from a registered building surveyor saying work built without a permit substantially complies with the building standards. You lodge it with a BA13 (houses, sheds, patios) or BA9 (other buildings) to get retrospective approval from council.

Last updated 15 November 2026

What a BA18 is

A certificate of building compliance (form BA18) is a signed statement from a registered building surveyor. For work that was built without a permit, it says the building substantially complies with each building standard that applies to it. It must come with plans and specifications that show how it complies.

The current form is dated 2 November 2023 in its footer. You can find it on the state's building approval forms page.

When you need one

You need a BA18 when you're asking council to approve work that's already been built without a permit:

Type of buildingForm you lodge with councilNeeds a BA18?
Class 1 and 10: houses, extensions, patios, sheds, carports, poolsBA13 building approval certificateYes
Class 2 to 9: units, shops, offices, factoriesBA9 occupancy permitYes

Step by step

  1. Check what's approved. Ask council for a plan search of your property. Anything with no approval record is treated as unauthorised. The City of Rockingham, for example, charges $60 for a full set of residential plans.
  2. Check if planning approval is needed first. Some councils need a retrospective planning or R-Codes approval before they'll issue the building approval. Rockingham says this for rural and special residential lots, and that lots zoned R2 to R80 may need an R-Codes application.
  3. Engage a registered building surveyor. They'll tell you what inspections and documents they need. A BA18 usually includes a site inspection.
  4. Gather the documents. Usually as-built plans (floor plan, elevations, sections, construction details), specifications and, where needed, an engineer's certificate for the structure.
  5. Get the BA18 signed. The surveyor checks the work against the building standards and signs the certificate.
  6. Lodge with council. Submit the BA13 (or BA9), the BA18 and every document listed on the BA18, and pay the fees.
  7. Council decides. If approved, council issues a building approval certificate (BA14), which lets the structure stay.

Who can issue it

Councils vary. The Shire of Mundaring says only a private building surveyor can issue a BA18. The City of Gosnells and the City of Rockingham offer a certification service and may be engaged to provide one. Rockingham's fee sheet lists a BA18 at a minimum of $400 for the first 3 hours, then $110 an hour. A list of registered building surveyors is on the state's Building and Energy website.

Council fees for the BA13

These are set by the state, so they're usually the same at every council:

FeeRate
BA13 for unauthorised Class 1 and 10 work0.38% of the value, minimum $121
Building services levy for unauthorised work0.274% of the value, minimum $123.30

That's about double the levy you'd pay if you got a permit first. See retrospective approval cost for worked examples.

If the work doesn't comply

Lodging doesn't guarantee approval. Council can ask for work to be done to bring it up to standard, refuse the application, or take action to have it removed. Building without a permit can carry fines of up to $50,000 for a first offence. There's no "7-year rule" that makes old work legal: read is there a 7-year rule in WA?

Questions

Common questions

What is a BA18 in WA?

A certificate of building compliance signed by a registered building surveyor. For unauthorised work, it states the building substantially complies with each building standard that applies, with plans and specifications showing how.

Who can issue a BA18 certificate?

A registered building surveyor. Councils vary: Mundaring says only a private surveyor can issue one, while Gosnells and Rockingham offer a certification service.

How much does a BA18 cost?

Private surveyors set their own prices. The City of Rockingham lists a minimum of $400 for the first 3 hours, then $110 an hour. Council's BA13 fee and levy are on top.

Do I need a BA18 for an unapproved patio or shed?

Yes. To get retrospective approval for Class 1 or 10 work like a patio or shed, you lodge a BA13 with a BA18 attached.
